Warehouse Operations & User Guide

Illustrated, step-by-step instructions for receiving, dispatching, transferring, and auditing warehouse stock.

Workflow 1
How to Receive & Add New Stock (+ Intake)

Follow these steps when new shipments or supplier deliveries arrive at the warehouse dock:

Step 1: Open Add Dialog
Click the green + Add Stock button in the top navigation bar, or press I on your keyboard.
Step 2: Select SKU & Location
Choose the item from the catalog dropdown and type the destination Bay / Bin (e.g. A-01-01).
Step 3: Enter Quantity & PO #
Enter the number of units received and type the Purchase Order or supplier reference note.
Step 4: Save & Confirm
Click Receive & Stock. On-hand units increment instantly, and an audit entry is automatically created.
Workflow 2
How to Pick & Dispatch Orders (− Dispatch)

Follow these steps when picking goods for customer shipments, work orders, or outbound deliveries:

Step 1: Locate Item
Find the item in the Inventory On Hand table using search or category filters.
Step 2: Click − Subtract
Click the red − Subtract button on the row or click the − quantity stepper.
Step 3: Enter Order Ref
Specify the units being picked and type the customer order or delivery tracking number.
Step 4: Dispatch
Click Dispatch Units. Stock levels decrease, and reorder warnings appear automatically if stock falls low.
Workflow 3
How to Move Stock Between Bays (⇄ Transfer)

Use location transfers when reorganizing racks or consolidating pallets into new bin locations:

Step 1: Click ⇄ Move
On the item's row, click the ⇄ Move button under Quick Actions.
Step 2: Enter Destination
Type the target location (e.g. from A-01-01 to B-03-02).
Step 3: Transfer & Confirm
Click Complete Transfer. The system automatically reduces from the source bay and adds to the destination bay.
Workflow 4
How to Perform a Physical Count Audit (⚙ Adjust)

Use count adjustment during regular cycle audits or year-end physical inventory reconciliations:

Step 1: Click ⚙ Adjust
Click ⚙ Adjust on the bay row you are physically counting.
Step 2: Enter Physical Count
Enter the actual count physically found on the rack and describe the reason for any variance.
Step 3: Save Count
Click Save Reconciled Count. The audit trail logs the exact delta variance and your operator name.
